## v2.1.19 (2026-11-08)

### Changed
- **Changing the location or DWD region in the web interface:** the cube now restarts by itself after saving. Before, the new location stayed invisible until you restarted the device by hand (the location is looked up at start). This mainly affects devices whose Wi-Fi was set up over the USB cable.
