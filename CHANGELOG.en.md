## v2.1.15 (2026-11-02)

### New
- **Privacy notice during set-up:** The set-up page in the set-up Wi-Fi now says before saving which services the device contacts (Open-Meteo, German Weather Service, update check at GitHub), that these see the IP address, and that the device reads no location. Plus a tick "Check for new firmware versions" (set by default): without the tick the device never asks for updates. Applies to new set-ups; devices that are already set up are unchanged, the setting stays in the web interface.
